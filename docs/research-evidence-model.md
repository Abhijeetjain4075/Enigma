# Enigma Research Evidence Model

## Why

"Research" becomes unreliable when facts, vendor claims, hypotheses and assumptions are mixed.

## Evidence classes

### E0 — assumption
An internal belief without external evidence.

### E1 — secondary research
Article, directory, analyst material or community report.

### E2 — primary public evidence
Official product documentation, standard, regulation or vendor statement.

### E3 — provider-confirmed evidence
Direct partner confirmation, written commercial/API confirmation or sandbox documentation.

### E4 — observed test
Reproducible technical test against a real or official sandbox.

### E5 — production evidence
Repeated real-world transaction evidence.

## Rule

Do not upgrade an E0-E2 claim into a production capability.

## Research record

Every material claim should record:

- claim
- source
- retrieval date
- evidence level
- affected component
- confidence
- counter-evidence
- next validation step

## Competitive claims

A competitor feature is only considered "verified" when a primary source or direct evidence supports the exact capability.

## Regulatory claims

Legal conclusions require jurisdiction-specific professional review. Public legal text establishes the rule; it does not automatically establish Enigma's exact legal role.
