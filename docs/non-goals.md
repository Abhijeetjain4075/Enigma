# Enigma Non-Goals

Enigma is not automatically:

- a charger manufacturer;
- an EV manufacturer;
- a physical charging-network owner;
- a generic consumer super-app;
- a payment bank;
- a universal energy retailer;
- an unlicensed payment intermediary;
- a replacement for OCPP;
- a replacement for OCPI;
- a replacement for existing roaming hubs;
- a guarantee of every provider's availability;
- a guarantee that every provider permits third-party transaction initiation.

The platform may integrate with these ecosystems where commercially, technically and legally permitted.

## Why this matters

A clear boundary prevents architecture from becoming a collection of unrelated features and prevents marketing claims from outrunning evidence.
