## Competitive neutralization mechanisms

A competitor can neutralize Enigma through:
- feature addition
- partnership
- standards adoption
- regulatory mandate
- open-source implementation
- data commoditization
- OEM bundling
- payment-network expansion
- roaming-hub expansion
- acquisition
- pricing compression
- vertical integration
- customer internal development

Every Enigma strategy must include a “how the market kills this” analysis.

## Evidence rule

Public evidence proves a public capability claim. It does not prove private contract terms, universal availability, reliability, market share, or superiority.

## Research targets

Continue investigating direct payment, ISO 15118 PKI, Plug & Charge roaming, tariff normalization, invoice reconciliation, cross-hub interoperability, contract automation, disputes, reliability scoring, queue prediction, reservations, battery swapping, V2G/DER, fleet authorization, parking/charging bundling, toll/charging orchestration, roadside/service integrations, open-source transaction engines and data licensing.

## Strategic rule

Do not count companies. Count substitute mechanisms.

The real competitor is the cheapest combination of systems a customer can assemble to solve the target workflow.
