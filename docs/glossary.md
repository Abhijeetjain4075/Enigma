# Enigma Glossary

## CPO
Charge Point Operator. Operates charging infrastructure.

## eMSP
e-Mobility Service Provider. Provides mobility/charging services to users and may enable access to charging networks.

## EVSE
Electric Vehicle Supply Equipment. The equipment/system used to supply electricity to an EV.

## CSMS
Charging Station Management System. Software that manages charging stations.

## Roaming
Interoperability allowing a user contracted with one mobility service provider to access infrastructure operated by another participant.

## OCPI
Open Charge Point Interface. An open interface used for CPO/eMSP interoperability and roaming.

## OCPP
Open Charge Point Protocol. Communication protocol between charging stations and charging management systems.

## OICP
Open InterCharge Protocol. A roaming protocol used in the Hubject ecosystem.

## ISO 15118
International standard family for communication between road vehicles and the grid/EVSE environment, including Plug & Charge-related functions.

## CDR
Charge Detail Record. A record describing a completed charging transaction for billing/reconciliation.

## NAP
National Access Point. A national mechanism for publishing/accessing transport/alternative-fuels data.

## Transaction
Enigma's commercial and operational envelope around one or more service operations.

## Operation
A concrete action requested from a provider, such as start, stop, reserve or refund.

## Evidence
An observation supporting a claim about state, capability, price, identity or outcome.

## Reconciliation
The process of comparing expected state or money with external evidence and resolving mismatches.

## Idempotency
A property allowing a repeated request to produce one logical effect rather than duplicate effects.

## eRoaming
Roaming between eMobility service providers and charge point operators.

## V2G / V2X
Vehicle-to-grid / vehicle-to-everything energy flows.

## DER
Distributed Energy Resources.

## Merchant of Record
The entity legally responsible for a transaction with the customer in the relevant commercial structure.

## Design Partner
An early customer or provider that collaborates on validating a product workflow before broad launch.
