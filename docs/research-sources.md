# Enigma Research Source Register

This file records authoritative or primary sources used for the architecture and standards audit.

## EV charging protocols

### Open Charge Alliance — OCPP
https://openchargealliance.org/protocols/

OCA currently documents OCPP 1.6, 2.0.1 and 2.1. OCPP 2.1 adds capabilities including bidirectional charging, DER control, battery swapping and expanded payment/authorization features.

### EV Roaming Foundation — OCPI
https://github.com/ocpi/ocpi
https://ocpi-protocol.com/

The official OCPI repository identifies OCPI 2.3.0 as the latest official release. OCPI covers modules including locations, tariffs, tokens, sessions, CDRs, commands, charging profiles and hub connectivity.

### Hubject — OICP/intercharge
https://www.hubject.com/intercharge-overview
https://www.hubject.com/company/download

Hubject documents intercharge as a roaming network connecting CPOs and eMSPs, with authorization and settlement capabilities, and publishes OICP product documentation.

### ISO — ISO 15118
https://www.iso.org/standard/77845.html

ISO 15118-20:2022 specifies second-generation EV/EVSE communication and supports bidirectional power transfer. The ISO catalogue also records a 2026 amendment.

## European regulation

### EUR-Lex — AFIR
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1804/oj/eng

Regulation (EU) 2023/1804 establishes requirements concerning alternative-fuels infrastructure, payment, user information and data access, including APIs and national access points.

## Privacy

### MeitY — India data protection
https://www.meity.gov.in/

Use the official ministry source for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and associated implementation material.

## Research rule

Primary sources should be preferred for protocol versions, regulatory requirements, certification, contractual requirements and official product capabilities.

Secondary sources may be used for market discovery, but a competitive or regulatory claim should be revalidated against a primary source before being treated as a launch-critical fact.

## Freshness

This register is dated 2026-09-30. Protocols, regulations, commercial offerings and APIs change. Every production integration needs a versioned evidence record with retrieval date.
